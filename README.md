# Bank of Hawaii (bank-of-hawaii)

Bank of Hawaii is a state-chartered commercial bank organized under the laws of the State of Hawaii in 1897, a Federal Reserve System member and FDIC-insured institution. It is the principal banking subsidiary of Bank of Hawaii Corporation (NYSE: BOH), a Honolulu-headquartered regional financial services company serving Hawaii, Guam, and other Pacific islands with consumer, commercial, and wealth-management banking.

## Open Finance / API Posture

Bank of Hawaii publishes no first-party public developer portal and exposes no documented public API. Probes of `developer.boh.com` and `developers.boh.com` do not resolve, and `www.boh.com/developer(s)` returns HTTP 404. This is the honest and common reality for regional US banks: US open finance is voluntary and fragmented, with no single mandated open-banking contract.

Consumer-permissioned access to Bank of Hawaii account data is available in practice only through third-party data aggregators (for example Plaid), not through a bank-operated API. No Financial Data Exchange (FDX) membership and no published CFPB Section 1033 (Personal Financial Data Rights) data-access posture were found at the time of this record. No downloadable OpenAPI or Swagger specification exists to harvest.

This record is therefore identity-only (a stub): it captures who the institution is and its genuine open-finance posture, without inventing any API surface.
